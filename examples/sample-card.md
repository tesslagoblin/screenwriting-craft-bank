# Endorse then stunt

**Show:** The Long Weekend · **Episode:** S01E04
**Tags:** status, misdirection, sibling-dynamics

**How it works:** A character who cannot win a comparison loudly agrees with it
instead, praising the person they are being measured against, and then
immediately does something outrageous that moves the room's attention onto
themselves. They never contest the ground they would lose on. They change which
ground everyone is standing on.

**Apply when:** You have a character who is second place at something permanent
and you do not want to write them as bitter. This gives them agency inside the
loss without ever naming it.

**Project relevance:** Works for any character living next to someone
unbeatable. The key is that the endorsement has to be sincere, or it reads as
sarcasm and the whole move collapses.

**Additional examples:** The same mechanic shows up in the second episode of
Harbour Road, where the younger brother toasts his sibling's promotion and then
sets fire to the garden.

## Where We Dug

The move is two beats and it only works because of the order. The endorsement
has to land first and has to be real.

We tested whether it reads as jealousy. It does not, because she never says
anything against the sister, and the audience is never given a line to point at
as the resentment. The wanting shows up only as behaviour.

> "she cant compete on pretty so she competes on chaos"

The texture question that cracked it was asking what the sister was doing while
it happened. The answer, laughing, is what makes it playable. If the sister were
annoyed it would be a rivalry scene. Because the sister enjoys it, it stays a
closeness scene with something uncomfortable underneath, and the audience has to
hold both.
